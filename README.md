# CaixaBank 2025 Term Deposit Marketing Campaign Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes CaixaBank 2025 Term Deposit Telemarketing campaign to understand customer subscription patterns and identify opportunities to improve term deposit marketing effectiveness.

Using data analytics, the project aims to transform campaign data into actionable business insights that support customer segmentation, campaign planning, and data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze customer characteristics and term deposit subscription patterns.
- Evaluate telemarketing campaign performance.
- Identify customer segments with stronger subscription potential.
- Examine campaign contact frequency and other relevant factors.
- Develop recommendations to improve marketing efficiency and resource allocation.

## Tools and Technologies

- **SQL Server / T-SQL:** Data validation, querying, and business analysis.
- **Python:** Data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and feature engineering.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and business intelligence reporting.

## Project Structure

- `data/` — Dataset storage; raw data may be included on Git.
- `notebooks/` — Jupyter notebooks for exploratory data analysis.
- `src/` — Python scripts for data preparation and feature engineering.
- `sql/` — SQL scripts for data validation and campaign analysis.
- `tableau/` — Tableau visualization and documentation.
- `powerbi/` — Power BI reports and documentation.
- `reports/` — Technical reports and business findings.
- `images/` — Dashboard screenshots and project visuals.
- `stakeholders presentation slide deck/` — Strategic roadmap and stakeholder action plan raw powerpoint slide.
- `slide deck images/`screenshots and visuals for stakeholders presentation slide deck.
- `docs/`raw documentation of business problem and project findings.

## Analytical Approach

1. Data understanding and validation.
2. Data cleaning and preparation.
3. Exploratory data analysis.
4. SQL-based customer and campaign analysis.
5. Power BI dashboard development.
6. Interpretation of findings and business recommendations.

## Expected Business Value

The analysis is intended to help marketing stakeholders better understand customer behavior, prioritize promising customer segments, and make more informed decisions about telemarketing campaigns.
